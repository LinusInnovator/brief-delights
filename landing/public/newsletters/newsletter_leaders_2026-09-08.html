AI's real risk isn't hype—it's silent ubiquity.

Everyone's debating whether AI is overhyped or underhyped, but the quieter story is how quickly it's becoming invisible infrastructure—from Meta's models to Kenya's gig economy. The dissenting view isn't that AI will fail, but that it will succeed too quietly, embedding itself into daily life and labor before we've agreed on the rules. That's the shift worth watching, not the next benchmark.
