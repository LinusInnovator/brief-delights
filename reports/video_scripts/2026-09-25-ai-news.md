# Story 1: DeltaWAM | Delta World Action Models for Bimanual Manipulation
### Landing Page
[confident] DeltaWAM rethinks world-action models by predicting only changed content, not dense future frames. That's a big efficiency win for robot control.

### Demo
[amazed] By dropping repetitive visual modeling, it decouples action dynamics from nuisance appearance. Bimanual manipulation gets cleaner, faster, and more generalizable.

# Story 2: ViRDM | Few-Step Causal Video Generation
### Paper
[curious] ViRDM asks a sharp question: do few-step autoregressive video diffusion models really need a giant teacher and an online critic?

### Architecture
[confident] Instead, it uses representation distribution matching, cutting the resource-heavy teacher-critic loop while keeping low-latency streaming generation intact.

### Release Horizon
[hopeful] Open weights are flagged on Hugging Face. If this holds up, real-time video gen just got cheaper for everyone.
